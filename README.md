## About Us
We are the IT Department of the Erasmus Student Network ASSI Parma. Our goal is to engineer automated solutions to replace manual workflows, making the life of our volunteers easier and improving the experience for international students.

## Tech Stack
Our tools and bots are primarily developed using:
* **Python** 
* **Java**
* **C/C++**

## Key Projects
* **Yapla Bot:** An automated tool designed to handle financial reconciliation, drastically reducing the workload for the treasury department.
* **JupiBot:** Automation scripts and integrations for managing local events and section operations.
